# 🎯 LeadScout AI - Desktop & Multi-Device Lead Intelligence Platform

Run LeadScout AI natively on **Mac (App Icon in Applications & Desktop), Windows PC, iPhone Safari, and Android Chrome**.

---

## 🍎 1-Click Mac Start & Automatic App Icon Installation

When you extract the project ZIP on your Mac:

1. **Double-click `START_MAC.command`** (or `start.command` / `start.sh`) inside the folder.
2. The script will automatically:
   - Verify Python and dependencies
   - **Install the native "LeadScout AI" App Icon** in your Mac's `/Applications` folder and on your **Desktop** (`~/Desktop/LeadScout AI.app`)
   - Launch the server and automatically open **LeadScout AI** in your default browser (`http://localhost:8501`)
3. **Simultaneous Multi-Device Access**:
   - Access on your Mac screen
   - Open on your iPhone or Android on the same Wi-Fi using the Network URL shown in the terminal (e.g., `http://192.168.x.x:8501`)
   - Any time in the future, just double-click the **LeadScout AI** app icon on your Mac desktop or in Launchpad!

---

## 🪟 Windows PC 1-Click Start

1. Double-click **`START_WINDOWS.bat`** (or `run_windows.bat`).
2. Your browser will automatically launch to `http://localhost:8501`.

---

## 📱 iPhone (Safari) & Android (Chrome) Simultaneous Access

1. Ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your Mac/PC.
2. Open Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android) and enter the `Network URL` displayed in your terminal (e.g. `http://192.168.1.45:8501`).
3. Tap **Share ➔ Add to Home Screen** on iPhone or **3 dots ➔ Install app / Add to Home Screen** on Android.
